Raccoon Diplomacy

We're all just raccoons helping other raccoons — and we know how to call for help from the people, if it comes to it.

We're a harm reduction group. That means we help people who use drugs stay alive and be safer, without asking them to stop first.

We're peers. Not clinicians, not counsellors, not anybody's licensing board. Lived experience counts as knowledge here, and nobody is speaking down at anyone.

The name is literal

Somebody said we should open diplomatic relations with the raccoons.

Then a friend went out at 3am and actually talked to one.

That's the whole origin story. We kept the name because it's true, and because it's funny, and funny is free.

Underneath the funny: the founder was on a substance, and it made him not sober. The Queen of Squirrels, from whom the story is better told, was sober, and that matters — her identity stays private, and she's earned the right to that.

Why raccoons

Resourceful. Adaptable. They work with whatever's available and they show up for each other in the dark.

And they have no shame whatsoever. A raccoon caught in your bin makes eye contact. It is not sorry.

Shame is the thing that stops people asking for help. So there isn't any here.

What we actually think

We won't ask you to quit. Stopping is a good outcome. So is using less, using more safely, or swapping something for something gentler. Those all count. You pick.

Nobody gets a lecture. No scare stories, no "have you tried just stopping." If you want to talk about what you use, we're interested. If you don't, that's fine too.

No question is too basic. There are questions we're all glad someone finally asked.

We're raccoons, and we know how to call the people. We look after each other — that's most of what we do. But we're not a hospital and we've never pretended to be. Knowing when to call for help isn't a failure of the raccoon way. It is the raccoon way. Diplomacy means having someone to ring.

One plain-words note: calling for help can bring police along, depending on where you are. The point of the call is that someone gets help, not that anyone gets in jail. DanceSafe and TripSit can tell you what the options look like where you are.

What we are right now

Small, and new. About twelve of us in Kansas City. We've met a few times, we have a Discord, and this page is most of our public presence.

We're the first Embassy — that's our word for a local group. There's one of them so far. We started with the local pagan community and we'd like to be useful to other alternative communities too.

We speak at places we've been invited to. We don't represent them, set their rules, or answer for them.

We'd like there to be more Embassies. That's a goal, not a description, and we'd rather tell you that than sound bigger than we are.

What we're not

Not a clinic. Not a charity with a board. Not medical or legal advice, and not a substitute for anyone who is.

For anything about doses or drug interactions, we point at DanceSafe and TripSit, who do that properly, rather than repeat their work with our name on it.

If this is about you

Our name came from a story a friend has been told so often she now has a title: the Queen of Squirrels. We don't use her name. If you recognise yourself and you'd rather not be part of this, say so in the Discord and we'll take it out. It's ours to tell, but it's hers to leave.

If something here is wrong

We've only been talking for a few months and none of this has been reviewed by a clinician. If you find something inaccurate or unsafe, tell us in the Discord and we'll change it. That's a better offer than it sounds — the people who use this are the people who can tell us when it's wrong.
