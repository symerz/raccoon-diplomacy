Safer Night Out

Draft, unreviewed. Peer-written, not medical advice. Correct us in the Discord if something here is wrong.

The night starts the day before

Most of what determines whether tonight goes well has already happened by the time you get dressed. Sleep. Eat. Start drinking water today, not when you arrive thirsty. If you're ill, feverish, or had a rotten week — a body under strain is a body at higher risk, and tonight is allowed to be a smaller night.

If you take prescribed medication, check how it interacts with anything you might use before you go — tripsit.me has a free interaction checker.

Decide now, because 2am you is not a planner

Pick your ceiling. Pick a time after which you are not redosing. Say both out loud to a friend. A decision spoken to another person is a different kind of decision than one you made privately and can quietly renegotiate at 1:40am.

Your kit is a pouch, not a rucksack

  • Test kit — reagents and fentanyl strips
  • Naloxone, and a rough idea of how to use it
  • Water — a refillable bottle
  • Electrolytes
  • Snacks — something dull and digestible
  • Earplugs — hearing damage is permanent and boring
  • Charger / power bank
  • A written meeting point and one emergency contact, on paper

Agree before you leave

Who your buddy is. Where you'll meet if phones die. When you'll check in.

Don't mix, and check

Combining substances is where people get hurt. Check tripsit.me before taking anything together. Nothing plus driving. Ever.

If someone's in trouble

Call 911. Stay with them. Tell the dispatcher what you know, then do what they say. Waiting to see if it gets better is what kills people. Poison Control 1-800-222-1222 · Crisis 988 · SAMHSA 1-800-662-4357.
